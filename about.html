About Me

Hi! I'm Loyd Ywen Masangcay. I was born on February 26, 2003, and my zodiac sign is Pisces. I am currently 23 years old and I live in Nancalobasaan, Urdaneta City, Pangasinan.

I am the eldest in our family and I have one younger sibling. Growing up, I developed an interest in creative work and digital media, especially editing pictures and videos.

I am a 4th year student at Urdaneta City University (UCU) taking the course Bachelor of Science in Information Technology. I am expected to graduate in 2026.

Currently, I am completing my On-the-Job Training (OJT) at the LGU Urdaneta City – City Library Department. My task involves encoding information from the library's vertical files.

I enjoy editing photos and videos and learning different digital skills. Someday, I hope to become a Graphic Designer and create visual designs that communicate ideas and creativity.

Fun Facts About Me

My first name "Loyd" was originally supposed to be spelled "Lloyd" with double L, but it was changed to a single L so that it would not be too common. The name "Ywen" has a special meaning because it came from my parents' call sign "We". The letter Y came from the last letter of my father's name Billy, while the letter N came from the first letter of my mother's name Nida. That is how the name Ywen was formed. One of my noticeable features is that I have a dimple, and I also have a small patch of white skin on my stomach which I was born with. My hobbies include editing pictures and videos, cooking, watching movies, and listening to music.
